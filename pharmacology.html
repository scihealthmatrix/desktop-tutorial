Introduction to Pharmacology

Pharmacology is the branch of science that studies drugs, their effects, mechanisms and uses in healthcare.

What is Pharmacology?

Pharmacology helps us understand how medicines interact with the human body and how they are used for treatment and prevention of diseases.

Drug Classification

  • Analgesics (Pain Relievers)
  • Antibiotics
  • Antihypertensive Drugs
  • Antidiabetic Drugs
  • Anti-inflammatory Drugs

How Drugs Work

Medicines produce effects by interacting with specific targets in the body such as receptors, enzymes and cells.

Importance in Healthcare

Knowledge of pharmacology helps healthcare professionals use medicines safely and effectively.

Conclusion

Pharmacology plays an important role in modern medicine and improves patient care through scientific understanding.
