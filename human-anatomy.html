Human Anatomy Basics

Human anatomy is the study of the structure of the human body and its different systems.

Introduction

The human body is made up of organs, tissues and cells that work together to maintain life.

Major Body Systems

  • Skeletal System
  • Muscular System
  • Nervous System
  • Cardiovascular System
  • Digestive System

Conclusion

Understanding anatomy helps students and healthcare professionals learn how the human body functions.
