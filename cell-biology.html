Cell Biology Fundamentals

Cell biology is the branch of science that studies the structure, functions and processes of cells.

What is a Cell?

A cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life. All living organisms are made up of cells.

Cell Structure

  • Cell Membrane
  • Cytoplasm
  • Nucleus
  • Mitochondria
  • Ribosomes

Cell Organelles

Organelles are specialized structures inside cells that perform different functions necessary for survival.

Cellular Functions

Cells perform important processes including energy production, protein synthesis, growth and reproduction.

Conclusion

Understanding cell biology provides the foundation for learning about health, disease and life sciences.
